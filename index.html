Import your history

Export your data from ChatGPT → Settings → Data controls → Export data, or Claude → Settings → Privacy → Export data. Unzip the download on your computer and select conversations.json. You can select several JSON files at once.

What this alpha keeps

Text messages, titles, dates and ChatGPT branches. The reader starts on the active branch; use its branch picker to see alternatives. Raw imported JSON is retained in library backups. Images, uploaded files, generated assets, rendered tools and shared links are not downloaded. Unsupported content is marked rather than silently presented as complete.

Your browser is the storage

Everything stays in IndexedDB on this browser and this site address. Clearing site data, switching browsers or private browsing can lose access. Export regular library backups and keep them somewhere safe. Backups contain private conversation text and raw export data.

Honest handoffs

Handoffs contain your selected excerpts, source IDs and an editable checklist. They are not AI summaries and do not decide what you agreed on. Threadkeep does not send messages to AI providers or delete chats on their services.

Moving between devices

Export library backup, open Threadkeep on the other device, and restore the backup. Markdown exports are for reading and handoffs; JSON backups preserve your library.
